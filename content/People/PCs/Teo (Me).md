I grew up in dense, untamed forest near a large port city, learning to walk silently on moss and track deer before I could read.

Survival here was brutal. But at some point, I realized that the city dwellers paid a fortune for trinkets that I found mundane or ordinary. At the start of adulthood, age 20, I left my parents abode and set out to make my own way. I began as a guide, leading the upper class on safaris, before realizing that the money was not in bringing the nobles to the wild, but in bringing the wild to the nobles.

I became a bit of an acquisition specialist (Taxa Factotum, Taxa Vigneron), not a common poacher but someone who could trap a displacer beast without ruining its pelt, or capture a flumpf without breaking its spirit.

My last job was to capture a live glass-scale hydra hatchling, whose scales reflected light like a beautiful chandelier. It was to be the centerpiece in the foyer of a nobleman's estate; Issath Rehtox, a tiefling aristocrat and merchant lord known for loving beauty and lacking empathy.

It took three months to track the creature down, losing two of my toes to frostbite. But ultimately, I returned to Issath, a reinforced glass cask containing the hatchling in hand.

We met on the docks outside of one of his warehouses. The payment was going to be enough to retire on, or at least disappear for a decade or two.

Issath inspected the merchandise, smiled, and nodded to his guards. He explained, with a cold, bored demeanor, that a dead smuggler tells no tales, and keeping the location of the Hydra's nest secret was worth more than my life. I didn't fight my way out. I was exhausted from the hunt. A crossbow bolt took me in the chest, and a dagger thrust through my chun up into my brain finished the job. I died on a cold stone floor, smelling of brine and alchemical preservatives.

Crikek was a Kenku hireling employed by Issath to do his odd jobs; today, she was tasked with dumping my body in the harbor. However, Crikek had been watching me; she admired my skill in the wild - something Kenku, who cannot fly, deeply envy.

Instead of the harbor, she dragged my corpse to a run-down temple of Mask. She offered the priest every copper she had scavenged, plus a stolen jeweled ring from Issath’s own desk, to cast Revivify.

It worked. I woke up gasping, adorned with new scars and cynicism in my heart. Crikek and I fled the city that night, and our sibling-like bond grew stronger. 

Our escape wasn’t a clean break; Issath’s reach was long, and for months we were less "travelers" and more "hunted game." We spent those weeks huddled in damp limestone caves and thickets, sharing thin blankets and thinner rations. It was during those long, paranoid nights that our bond solidified. I taught her how to mask her scent and step without clicking her talons, while she kept my spirits from curdling into total darkness.

During our trek, Crikek’s fascination with my trophies turned toward the scientific. She wasn't just interested in the plants I gathered, but the volatile reactions they could produce when refined. She spoke of an alchemical school where she could master the elements, a dream far removed from the blood and brine of Issath’s warehouses. We realized our best chance was a pincer move: I would return to the shadows as a mercenary to gain connections and power in order to systematically dismantle Issath’s empire and claim his wealth, while she sought safety to begin her studies.


I am currently working odd mercenary jobs, gathering coin and intel in order to burn Issath’s influence to the ground and strip him of every copper he owns. My ultimate goal is to reunite with Crikek once the coast is clear, using the merchant lord’s own fortune to fund her alchemical academy. Until then, we remain close in spirit, two outcasts working toward a day when the forest and the lab are no longer places to hide, but places to call home.


Mastermind subclass? 2014

  

Soulknife (2024) magic ideas:

  

1. The "Phantom Limb" of the Mind

The dagger went through my jaw and into my brain. While Revivify knit the flesh back together, maybe it left a "scar" on my psyche.

- I suffer from phantom pains in my jaw or sudden, ringing headaches when I’m stressed. I might occasionally feel a tingling in my palm where a weapon should be.
    
- In a moment of desperation, I reach for a dagger that isn't there, and my brain, remembering the sensation of the blade that killed it, projects one into my hand.
    
- My psychic blades look like jagged shards of blood-stained iron, mimicking the very dagger Issath’s guard used to "finish" me.
    

2. The Grace of Mask

Crikek took me to a temple of Mask (the God of Shadows and Thieves). Gods rarely do favors for free.

- Shadows seem to cling to me a little longer than they should. I feel a strange "static" in the air when I’m sneaking.
    
- My powers are a gift (or a curse) from the Shadow Lord. My Psychic Whispers are literally "Shadow-speak," and my blades are made of solidified shadow or something.
    
- Mask wants Issath’s wealth and secrets just as much as I do. My psychic blades are the tools Mask has "lent" me to collect that debt.
